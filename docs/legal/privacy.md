## The short version

Readable works fully without an account. Your books stay on your device unless you choose to sign in and sync.

## What we store

- **Without an account:** nothing leaves your device.
- **With an account:** your email address, optional display name, and your synced library: book titles and details, the original book files you import, reading progress, bookmarks, shelves, settings and reading statistics.

## What we do with it

We use it only to run sync across your devices. We don't sell it, and we don't use it for advertising.

## Where it lives

Account data is held with our backend provider (Supabase). Book files are stored privately; only your account can read them.

## Your choices

- **Delete your account** any time from Settings → Account. This permanently removes your account, your synced data and your uploaded book files from our servers. Books already on your devices stay on those devices.
- You can use Readable without an account.
